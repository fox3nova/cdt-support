What CDT does

CDT helps flight crew review duty, rest, flight time, LBO, Live Activity reminders, and Apple Watch companion information from user-entered route and settings data.

Local-first workflow

Core calculations run on device and do not require an account or server sign-in.

Operational boundaries

CDT is a planning and reference tool. Final legality must be confirmed against company manuals and applicable regulations.
