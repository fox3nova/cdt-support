Release Notes

Public update history for CDT.

Version 1.0

Initial App Store release

May 2026
  • Duty and flight-time reference calculation for short-term crew planning.
  • Route workflow with STD, STA, planned flight time, ACT OUT, and ACT IN fields.
  • Configurable company-style planning settings for report, taxi, duty, flight-time, rest, and LBO values.
  • Live Activity and local notification stages for REPORT, STD, DLY, and LBO reminders.
  • Apple Watch companion view for quick duty snapshot review and time entry.
  • In-flight rest planning for multi-crew and double-crew duty references.
  • Rolling 24-hour flight-time reference and cabin-limit reference display.
  • Offline-oriented manual and local profile import/export workflow.
